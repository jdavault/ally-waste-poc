✦ Based on my audit of https://allywaste.com/, the Ally Waste brand uses a professional "Eco-Tech" color palette characterized by high-contrast
  dark navy and vibrant green.

  Primary Brand Colors
   * Logo Green (#7EB141): The primary action color. Used for the logo's "leaf" element, call-to-action buttons (e.g., "Request a Quote"), and
     key icons.
   * Logo Navy (#101A30): The primary anchor color. Used for the website header, footer, and deep-background sections. This provides a high-end,
     corporate feel.

  Supporting UI Palette
   * Vivid Accent Green (#00D084): A brighter green often used for interactive states like button hovers and success indicators.
   * Pure White (#FFFFFF): The main background for content sections, ensuring clean readability.
   * Soft Gray (#F1F3F5): Used for "zebra-striping" or alternating sections to break up long pages visually.
   * Typography Dark (#32373C / #313131): A softened dark gray used for body text to reduce eye strain compared to pure black.

  Visual Identity Summary
  The palette strikes a balance between Logistics/Trust (the deep navy) and Sustainability/Growth (the greens). For your POC, I recommend using
  the Navy (#101A30) for your dashboard's sidebar/header and the Green (#7EB141) for your primary action buttons and "service completed" status
  indicators.
